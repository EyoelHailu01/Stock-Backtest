METHODOLOGY

Execution model

Moving averages use only prior closing prices. Both strategies begin after the same warm-up period and exit at the final close. Fees apply to every buy and sell.

See model assumptions

Long-only, one stock, fractional shares, and all available cash invested. No shorting or leverage. Dividends, taxes, spread, and slippage are excluded. Use consistently split-adjusted Open and Close prices. The fee is a percentage of transaction value. Drawdown includes the starting balance and daily closing equity. Trade win rate counts completed buy/sell pairs after fees. Historical results are simulated, not a prediction.
